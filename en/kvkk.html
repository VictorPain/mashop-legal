PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION NOTICE UNDER LAW NO. 6698 ("KVKK")

Data Controller: Fatih Acı Contact: fatihaci79@gmail.com Date: 06/10/2026

This notice has been prepared pursuant to Article 10 of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 of the Republic of Turkey ("KVKK").

Data Controller / Data Processor Distinction. maShop (Fatih Acı) acts as a data controller for the data it collects for authentication, subscription and device management. By contrast, with respect to the customer, supplier and personnel personal data that users enter into the app themselves, maShop acts as a data processor within the meaning of Art. 3 KVKK; the data controller for such data is the user who manages the relevant store account. The obligations to provide a privacy notice, establish a lawful basis and respond to data subject requests for this third-party data belong to the user; maShop stores/processes such data solely on the user's behalf and under their instructions, and does not use it for its own purposes.

3. Method of Collection and Legal Basis

Your personal data is collected via your login and registration actions within the app, Google or Apple Sign-In (OAuth) and automatic system records (Firebase).

Legal bases:

  • KVKK Art. 5/2-c (conclusion and performance of a contract): The core function of the app, the cross-device synchronisation and backup service, and the delivery of in-app notifications fall under this basis.
  • KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest): Error monitoring and performance analysis (Firebase) fall under this basis. In addition, non-personalized, opt-in rewarded ads (Google AdMob) shown to sustain the free plan fall under this basis; as the ads are not personalized, no profiling is performed and the user is free to choose whether to watch an ad.

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject (KVKK Article 11)

Pursuant to Article 11 of KVKK you have the following rights:

  • To learn whether your personal data has been processed
  • To request information about processing, if any
  • To request the deletion or destruction of your data
  • To request restriction of processing
  • To request that the processing be notified to third parties to whom the data has been transferred
  • To object to consequences against you resulting from analysis carried out by automated systems

Data Portability (Art. 11/ğ — automated as of V1.0): Active users and users within the recovery (grace) period may obtain a machine-readable copy of their data via the in-app Download My Data button in JSON format. After access has been fully removed, requests are made by email.

Application: You may apply by email to fatihaci79@gmail.com. Applications are answered within 30 days.

5. Data Retention Periods

  • Transaction data (device): Until you delete it from your device
  • Transaction data (server copy): For as long as your account remains open; permanently deleted upon an account deletion request (at the end of the 30-day grace period) or after 1 year of inactivity
  • Deleted record marker (tombstone): When you delete a record, it is kept marked as "deleted" on the server for 30 days so the deletion can propagate to your other devices, then permanently removed; during this period the record is not displayed and is excluded from reports
  • Google Sign-In: Until the account is closed
  • Firebase log data: According to Firebase policy (typically 90 days)
  • Push notification token: Deleted from our server when you log out or when your account is deleted
  • Personnel / commission data: For as long as the account remains open (subject to the same rules as transaction data)
  • Account deletion request: A 30-day grace period after the request; at the end all account data (personal + transaction) is permanently deleted
  • Inactive account: If no login occurs for a continuous 1 year, the account and all its data are permanently deleted (with prior notice)
  • Deletion audit record: Deletion date + number of deleted records; kept under KVKK Art. 7/12 (contains no content/personal data)
  • Security audit log: 365 days, then automatically deleted
  • No retention such as 10 years is applied for commercial records (the Article 82 TCC obligation belongs to the user)

6. Synchronisation, Backup and Multi-User Services

Synchronisation and Backup (all plans):

  • Your transaction data is stored primarily on your device and, by the nature of the service (preventing data loss on device change, multi-user / multi-device synchronisation, backup), is synchronised on all plans with our own server in Turkey (Muğla) over an encrypted connection (TLS 1.3); the server-side backup is stored encrypted with AES-256.
  • Data is not transferred abroad; KVKK Art. 9 cross-border transfer does not apply to this storage.
  • Server-side data is subject to the retention rules in Section 5 (permanently deleted upon an account deletion request or after 1 year of inactivity).

Multi-User (invited partner / employee):

  • The primary user may invite another user by sharing an invitation link via WhatsApp / SMS.
  • The invited user accesses the same store account and works in sync (full or restricted access depending on their role).
  • A user in the Employee role only sees their own sales records and commission calculation; they cannot see the store's total turnover or purchase prices.
  • Before accepting the invitation, the invited user is shown this KVKK notice in the app and their acknowledgement is recorded; data sharing through the invitation link is approved at that point.

Termination of Working Relationship and Data Requests (2026-05-21):

  • The primary user (store owner) may terminate the access of an invited user in two ways:
    1. Standard termination: Access is restricted to their own sales history and own payment/receivable balance (read-only) for 14 days. At the end of 14 days the account is closed entirely.
    2. Immediate termination: Access is cut off at the same moment (for cases such as fraud or theft).
  • In addition, when the primary user's subscription ends, the system automatically closes the invited user's access after 14 days; if the subscription is renewed within this period, access is reopened.
  • Data entered into the app by the user whose access was terminated (sales they have made, commission records kept under their name) is not deleted; it is retained as sales history in the store owner's reports — this falls under KVKK Art. 5/2-c (mandatory record arising from the performance of a contract).
  • Personal data request (KVKK Art. 11): A user whose access has been terminated may apply by email to the data controller address above to request a copy of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of their personal data (sales records, commission calculation, payment history). Requests are answered within 30 days.

6.1 Partner Role and Asymmetric Authority (as of V1.0)

The maShop account has a multi-user structure consisting of the Primary Account Holder (founder) who created the account and the Partner / Employee role users they have invited.

Allocation of Authority:

  • Primary Account Holder: All authority over account deletion, subscription cancellation, ownership transfer, removal of other users and adjusting their permissions rests entirely with this user.
  • Partner: Equivalent data access to the owner (sales, purchases, expenses, customers, products); no account management authority.
  • Employee: Access only to records of their own sales; analytics, personnel and account management are closed.

Data Visibility and Responsibility:

Users in the Partner role have access to all commercial data within the account (past and future) from the moment the invitation is accepted. This access starts with the consent of the Primary Account Holder and may be restricted or terminated at any time.

Users in the Employee role have access only to records of their own sales and their own commission report.

Account Deletion and Notification:

When the Primary Account Holder requests account deletion:

  • The account enters soft delete (pending deletion confirmation) status and a 30-day grace period begins.
  • An in-app push notification is sent to active partner and employee users of the account.
  • During this period the Primary Account Holder may withdraw the deletion request.
  • When 30 days have passed, all account data is permanently deleted (hard delete).

In addition, if no user logs in for a continuous 1 year, the account is permanently deleted due to inactivity (with reasonable prior notice); if a login occurs within this period the period restarts. In no case does maShop continue to store deleted data on the user's behalf.

If partners or employees suffer data loss as a result of account deletion, they must direct their claims to the Primary Account Holder; maShop assumes no liability in this regard.

Conflict of Data Subject Rights (KVKK Art. 11):

A user's request to have their personal data deleted under KVKK Art. 11 is evaluated independently of any objections by other users (partner or employee). When an employee/partner is removed from an account, the transaction records they entered are retained as part of the store owner's records (KVKK Art. 5/2-c — mandatory record arising from the performance of a contract); this does not mean maShop retains the data "for 10 years under Article 82 TCC" — no such retention is applied. When the Primary Account Holder deletes the entire account, all data including commercial records is permanently deleted from the server at the end of the grace period; only an audit record of the deletion (date, number of deleted records) remains under KVKK Art. 7 and 12.

Audit Log Retention:

Security audit records of account management events (invitation, acceptance, role change, removal, account deletion) are retained for 365 days (1 year) for the purpose of KVKK Art. 12 accountability and security, then automatically deleted. They are not intended for commercial document retention.

Communication Channel:

Account management notifications (deletion, removal, permission change) are primarily delivered as in-app push notifications. A permanent channel (email) integration will be added in subsequent versions; until then, the user must periodically open the app for critical notifications.

7. Data Security Measures

Technical and administrative measures taken under KVKK Art. 12:

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
  • Backup encryption: AES-256 (at rest)
  • Access control: The server is accessed only by authorised personnel (the developer); the home server is protected by physical access restrictions
  • Network security: Firewall, fail2ban, Cloudflare proxy
  • Backup: Regular backups to multiple local media + cold storage
  • Data breach notification: Notification to the Authority and affected data subjects within 72 hours under KVKK Art. 12/5
