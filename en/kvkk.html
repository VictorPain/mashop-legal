2.1 Anonymised and Aggregated Data

maShop may make use of anonymised and aggregated data in order to improve the service and to produce sector benchmarking and recommendation features.

Anonymisation means rendering the data such that it can no longer be associated in any way with an identified or identifiable natural person. In this context the user name, email address, business name, customer/supplier/personnel names and account identifiers are removed; the data is used only in groups containing a sufficient number of accounts (for example, "average profit margin by product group"). Aggregation is never carried out at a level that would allow an individual account, business or person to be distinguished.

The daily totals kept to operate and plan the service (numbers of accounts and users, numbers of active users, plan distribution, platform and major operating-system version distribution, activation rate and similar) also fall under this section: they contain no account or personal identifier, values based on fewer than 5 accounts are not kept separately, and the activation rate is calculated over 7-day and 30-day periods rather than per day.

Anonymised data loses its character as personal data within the meaning of Law No. 6698 and therefore falls outside the scope of the Law (Art. 3/1-b definition, Art. 7/3). As anonymisation is irreversible, requests for erasure of or access to such data cannot be fulfilled; however, all of your rights under Section 4 remain fully applicable to your personal data prior to anonymisation.

Your raw transaction data (sales, purchases, expenses, customer, supplier and personnel records) is processed only to provide the service to you (storage, synchronisation, backup); it is not used for any other purpose, including model training, without anonymisation, and is not transferred to third parties. Record contents are not viewed in support or security reviews. The content-free audit trail showing which user changed your records and when is examined only in the context of a related support request or security incident; the technical trace of records not accepted during synchronisation (submission rejection trace) is used to detect and fix submission errors (Section 1).

5. Data Retention Periods

  • Transaction data (device): Until you delete it from your device

  • Deleted record marker (tombstone): When you delete a record, it is kept marked as "deleted" on the server for 30 days so the deletion can propagate to your other devices, then permanently removed; during this period the record is not displayed and is excluded from reports
  • Google Sign-In: Until the account is closed
  • Apple Sign-In: Until the account is closed. When an account deletion request is made, Apple's auth/revoke endpoint is called at the end of the 30-day grace period and the app is removed automatically from "Apps Using Apple ID" in your Apple ID settings. The same call is made when the account is deleted due to inactivity; in both cases your maShop sign-in record (Firebase identity) is also deleted.
  • Firebase log data: According to Firebase policy (typically 90 days)
  • Push notification token: Deleted from our server when you log out or when your account is deleted
  • Personnel / commission data: For as long as the account remains open (subject to the same rules as transaction data)
  • Account deletion request: A 30-day grace period after the request; at the end all account data (personal + transaction) is permanently deleted
  • Inactive account: If no active user of the account signs in for 365 days (1 year), the account and all its data are permanently deleted. For this rule, "signing in" means opening the app, or bringing it to the foreground, while connected to the internet; if your session is open you do not need to sign in again. When about 335 days have passed since the last sign-in, the account enters a warning stage and a push notification is sent to the account's active users; the notification may not reach you if notification permission is off, the session has been signed out, the device has changed, or the operating system has suspended apps that have not been used for a long time. The account is not deleted until at least 30 days have passed since the warning stage began; signing in during this period stops the deletion and restarts the period. Accounts with an active paid subscription are not deleted under this rule while the subscription continues; after the subscription ends, deletion can take place only after the same warning and waiting period. The app does not send notifications by email
  • Deletion proof record: When an account is deleted (on request or due to inactivity), the account identifier, an irreversible digest of the account holder's email address generated with a secret key (the plain email address is not stored), the request and deletion dates, the number of deleted records and the reason for deletion are kept for 10 years so that the deletion can be proven (KVKK Art. 5/2-e); it contains no transaction content
  • Security audit log: 365 days, then automatically deleted
  • Feedback and support requests: Requests marked "completed" are automatically deleted 1 year after they were created; open requests and requests under review are kept while your account remains open. They are deleted immediately when your account is deleted or when your membership of the account ends and your personal data is destroyed. They are not deleted when server backup is turned off
  • Privacy notice acknowledgement records: Kept until your account is deleted or, when your membership of the account ends, until your personal data is destroyed. They are not deleted when server backup is turned off
  • Submission rejection trace: The technical trace of a record not accepted during synchronisation is automatically deleted 90 days after that record was last rejected; when the account is deleted (at the end of the grace period or due to inactivity) it is deleted together with the account's other data
  • Server records (logs): The application server's records are automatically deleted after 30 days and the access records at the server entry point after at most 15 days; structured error records that cannot be associated with a person or an account are automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Device records: Until your membership or your account is deleted; records of devices that have been disconnected are also kept for this period
  • Administrator action record: 365 days, then automatically deleted; deleted together with the account's other data when the account is deleted
  • Announcement read record: For as long as your account remains open; permanently deleted together with the account upon account deletion (at the end of the 30-day grace period) or inactivity deletion
  • When server backup is turned off: The transaction-data copy on the server and the audit records relating to that data are permanently deleted 30 days after the date you turned backup off (the deletion job runs once a day, so deletion takes place after the 30 days have elapsed). They are not deleted if you turn backup back on within that period. Security audit records of account management events remain subject to the 365-day rule (Section 6.1). Your account, membership, device, subscription and consent records, your push notification token, the feedback you have sent, any partnership share/period records, your announcement read records and the record of your server-backup preference are not deleted; nor is the submission rejection trace deleted when backup is turned off — it is deleted automatically once its 90-day period has elapsed
  • System backups: Data that has been permanently deleted may remain in our server's encrypted system backups until it ages out of the backup rotation schedule (daily / weekly / monthly tiers; longest tier 12 months). These backups are accessed for disaster recovery purposes only; they cannot be viewed through the app and are not used in ordinary processing activities. This rule applies to all deletions, including account deletion
  • No retention such as 10 years is applied for commercial records (the Article 82 TCC obligation belongs to the user)

6.1 Partner Role and Asymmetric Authority (as of V1.0)

The maShop account has a multi-user structure consisting of the Primary Account Holder (founder) who created the account and the Partner / Employee role users they have invited.

Allocation of Authority:

  • Primary Account Holder: All authority over account deletion, subscription cancellation, ownership transfer, removal of other users and adjusting their permissions rests entirely with this user.
  • Partner: Equivalent data access to the owner (sales, purchases, expenses, customers, products); no account management authority.
  • Employee: Access only to records of their own sales; analytics, personnel and account management are closed.

Data Visibility and Responsibility:

Users in the Partner role have access to all commercial data within the account (past and future) from the moment the invitation is accepted. This access starts with the consent of the Primary Account Holder and may be restricted or terminated at any time.

Users in the Employee role have access only to records of their own sales and their own commission report.

Account Deletion and Notification:

When the Primary Account Holder requests account deletion:

  • The account enters soft delete (pending deletion confirmation) status and a 30-day grace period begins.
  • An in-app push notification is sent to active partner and employee users of the account.
  • During this period the Primary Account Holder may withdraw the deletion request.
  • When 30 days have passed, all account data is permanently deleted (hard delete).

In addition, if no active user of the account signs in for 365 days, the account is permanently deleted due to inactivity; the definition of "signing in", the prior notification, the waiting period of at least 30 days and the exemption for accounts with an active paid subscription are set out in Section 5. A sign-in by any active user within this period restarts the period. In no case does maShop continue to store deleted data on the user's behalf.

If partners or employees suffer data loss as a result of account deletion, they must direct their claims to the Primary Account Holder; maShop assumes no liability in this regard.

Conflict of Data Subject Rights (KVKK Art. 11):

A user's request to have their personal data deleted under KVKK Art. 11 is evaluated independently of any objections by other users (partner or employee). When an employee/partner is removed from an account, the transaction records they entered are retained as part of the store owner's records (KVKK Art. 5/2-c — mandatory record arising from the performance of a contract); this does not mean maShop retains the data "for 10 years under Article 82 TCC" — no such retention is applied. When the Primary Account Holder deletes the entire account, all data including commercial records is permanently deleted from the server at the end of the grace period; only the deletion proof record that demonstrates the deletion remains (its content and retention period: Section 5).

Audit Log Retention:

Security audit records of account management events (invitation, acceptance, role change, removal, account deletion) are retained for 365 days (1 year) for the purpose of KVKK Art. 12 accountability and security, then automatically deleted. They are not intended for commercial document retention.

Audit records relating to your transaction data (sales, purchases, expenses, products and similar records) follow the same rule as that data: if you turn server backup off, they are deleted together with the transaction data after 30 days (Section 5). Records of account management events are unaffected and complete the full 365-day period.

Communication Channel:

Account management notifications (deletion, removal, permission change, inactivity warning) are delivered as in-app push notifications; the app does not send notifications by email. Push notifications may not arrive if notification permission is off or the session has been signed out; you are therefore advised to open the app regularly for critical notifications.

7. Data Security Measures

Technical and administrative measures taken under KVKK Art. 12:

  • Encryption in transit: All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS); certificates are renewed automatically
  • Network security: The server's firewall rejects incoming connections by default; only the web ports (80/443) are open to the internet
  • Administrative access: Administrative access to the server (SSH) is possible only from the developer's device, with a cryptographic key rather than a password, and only over the local network or an encrypted private network (VPN); no administrative port is open on the router
  • Administration screen: Only the developer can access the service's administration screen, only over an encrypted private network (VPN) and with authorisation verified on the server side; the content of your transaction records is not shown on this screen and every administrator action is recorded
  • Rate limiting: The server applies per-user and per-IP request limits to restrict abuse and brute-force attempts
  • Backup: The database is backed up hourly and daily to two separate storage units (one of them on a separate device); backup completion is monitored automatically and restoration is tested periodically
  • Data breach notification: Notification to the Authority and affected data subjects within 72 hours under KVKK Art. 12/5
