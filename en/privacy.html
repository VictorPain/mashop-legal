PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 29/08/2026
Developer: Fatih Acı
Contact: fatihaci79@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how maShop ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Google Sign-In (Optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google solely for authentication purposes.

Apple Sign-In (Optional)
If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address from Apple solely for authentication purposes. If you select Apple's "Hide My Email" option, your real email address is replaced by an anonymous relay address (xxx@privaterelay.appleid.com); messages we send are forwarded to your real address through Apple's servers.

Data You Enter in the App
Your transaction records (product names, prices, dates, supplier/customer names) are stored primarily on your device and, by the nature of the service (preventing data loss on device change, multi-user / multi-device support, backup), are synchronised with our own server in Turkey (Muğla) over an encrypted connection. This data is not transferred abroad, is not shared with third parties, and is permanently deleted from the server when your account is deleted (see Section 7).

Technical Data (Firebase)
Firebase is used for authentication (login/session), in-app notifications (FCM push) and limited usage analytics. Analytics processes events such as session start, screen views and feature interactions, together with an app-instance identifier, in order to measure which parts of the app are used and improve the service. No interest profile is built from this data and it is not used for ad personalization. Crash reporting is not collected.

Payment Information
Subscription payments are collected directly by Google Play or the App Store (the relevant store acts as the merchant of record). We do not receive, process, or store your payment card or bank account information; this payment transfer is not performed by maShop.

Subscription Verification (RevenueCat)
We use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) to verify your subscription status and determine your plan entitlements (seat count, feature access). Only a user identifier derived from your in-app account and the subscription/purchase status received from the store are transmitted to this service. Your name, email address, payment card details and the business data you enter in the app are not transmitted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)
On the free (Free) plan, optional, opt-in rewarded video ads may be shown to unlock access to certain features. Ads are non-personalized: no interest profile is built and no behavioral targeting is performed. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may process your device advertising identifier (Android: Advertising ID / iOS: IDFA) and the approximate region derived from your IP address, for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. This does not mean your device's GPS location is accessed (see Section 2). No continuous or automatic banner ads are shown; an ad plays only when you choose to watch it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts / address book
  • Payment card or bank account details
  • Files or media on your device

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain core app functionality
  • To detect and fix technical errors
  • To verify subscription status

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. The advertising provider (AdMob) processes only limited technical data to serve non-personalized ads; your personal data is not sold for advertising purposes.

Potential risks of cross-border transfer (KVKK Art. 9/6-a): Several of the providers above are located abroad (USA / EU). In the country to which your data is transferred there may not be a level of data protection equivalent to KVKK, and it may become more difficult for you to exercise your rights as a data subject. Non-mandatory transfers (e.g. advertising) rely solely on your explicit consent, which you may withdraw at any time from the app settings. Transfers that are mandatory for the service to function, such as login and notifications, are necessary for you to use the app.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored on your device within the operating system's protected app sandbox. All data transfer with our server is encrypted with TLS 1.3 (HTTPS); server-side backups are stored encrypted with AES-256. Communications with Google services use HTTPS encryption.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under 18.

7. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes the data stored locally on your device; to also delete the synchronised copy on our server, simply submit an account deletion request from within the App. In addition, if no login occurs for a continuous 1 year, the account and all its data are permanently deleted (with prior notice).

Account Deletion: The in-app "Delete My Account" action permanently deletes your account after a 30-day grace period. For users signed in with Apple, this also invokes Apple's auth/revoke endpoint, causing the app to be removed automatically from "Apps Using Apple ID" in your Apple ID settings.

For deletion of Google Sign-In data or other requests, contact us at fatihaci79@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through an in-app notification. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
