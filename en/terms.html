Article 3 — User Data and Privacy

For our privacy practices please review the Privacy Policy.

3.1 Backup and Multi-Device Behaviour. User data is primarily stored on the device (local SQLite); server-side backup and cross-device synchronisation vary by your plan:

  • Free and Solo plans operate under a single-device policy. When you log in from a new device, the session on the previous device is automatically terminated. This behaviour does not cause data loss; the latest server-side backup is preserved.
  • The Pro plan supports concurrent access from multiple devices. The same account may be active on more than one device; data is automatically synchronised across devices. The user may view active devices via Settings → Connected Devices and manually remove any device.

3.2 Retention of Deleted Records (Tombstone). When a record is deleted, the record is kept on the server marked as "deleted" for 30 days to ensure deletion information reaches other devices. At the end of 30 days it is permanently deleted. This period is a technical necessity and is deemed under KVKK to constitute the end of processing; the record is not displayed and is not included in reports.

3.3 Account Deletion and Retention — KVKK Article 11. Your account and data are permanently deleted in two cases:

  • (a) Upon your deletion request: a 30-day grace period begins, during which you may recover your account. At the end of the period, all data belonging to the account (personal data and all transaction records including sales, purchases, expenses, cash and personnel) is permanently deleted (hard delete) from the server.
  • (b) Due to inactivity: if no login occurs for a continuous 1 year, the account and all its data are permanently deleted. You are notified a reasonable time before deletion; if you log in within this period your account is preserved and the period restarts (for seasonal use, if you return within the year you continue where you left off).

In both cases maShop does not continue to store this data on the user's behalf; there is no retention such as 10 years for commercial records. Your own retention obligations arising from tax and commercial legislation (including Article 82 of the Turkish Commercial Code) are yours; therefore, before deletion, you are advised to take a copy of your records via Settings → Download My Data. Deletion is irreversible. Only an audit record of the deletion event (date and number of deleted records) is kept under KVKK Articles 7 and 12.

Article 8 — Multi-User and Authority Balance

8.1 Primary Account Holder. The user who first opens the account and pays the subscription fee is defined as the Primary Account Holder. Deletion of the account, cancellation of the subscription, account transfer (to be implemented in future) and removal of invited users may be carried out only by the Primary Account Holder.

8.2 Invited Users. The Primary Account Holder may invite additional users (Partner or Employee) to their account. Acceptance of the invitation expresses the invited user's express consent to this Agreement and to the KVKK Notice.

8.3 Asymmetric Authority. A user in the Partner role has data access equivalent to that of the Primary Account Holder but does not have account management authority. The Primary Account Holder may individually restrict the permissions granted to a partner (viewing analytics, deep analytics, personnel page, cash movement deletion).

8.4 Account Deletion Flow. An account deletion request by the Primary Account Holder starts a 30-day grace period. During this period the account is in soft delete; when the period expires all account data is permanently deleted. In addition, if no login occurs for a continuous 1 year, the account is permanently deleted due to inactivity in accordance with clause 3.3(b) (with prior notice). An in-app push notification is sent to partners and employees; they have no active right of objection. Any dispute over data loss is between the Primary Account Holder and the partner/employee; maShop is not a party to such disputes.

8.5 Disputes and Wind-Up. In the event of a commercial partnership or personal dispute between a Partner and the Primary Account Holder, maShop remains neutral and does not provide any direction in favour of any party on the account. Management of data/financial matters of the account is resolved by an agreement (if any) between the parties themselves or by a court decision.

8.6 Data Subject Rights (KVKK Art. 11). A user's request to have their own personal data deleted is honoured independently of any objection by the other users of the account. When the Primary Account Holder deletes the account, all account data including commercial records is permanently deleted at the end of the grace period; maShop does not continue to store this data on the user's behalf. Retention obligations arising from tax/commercial legislation belong to the user, and an export option is provided before deletion. With respect to third-party personal data (customers, suppliers, personnel) that the user enters into the app, maShop acts as a data processor and the user as the data controller; the obligations to provide a privacy notice and a lawful basis for such data belong to the user (see the KVKK Personal Data Notice).

8.7 Ownership Transfer (feature postponed to future versions). Transfer of the Primary Account Holder role to another user (due to death, retirement or personal preference) is not possible in-app in this version; it will be added in subsequent versions through a "heir appointment" mechanism. Until then, in the event of loss of access by the account holder, the user must contact maShop support directly.

8.8 Neutrality of the Service Provider. maShop does not mediate in disputes between users within an account. Decisions taken by the Primary Account Holder are deemed binding by maShop.
