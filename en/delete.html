Account and Data Deletion

App: maShop (com.fatihaci.mashoppro) Developer: Fatih Acı Last updated: 26/07/2026

There are two ways to delete your maShop account and all data associated with it. You can use the second option (email) even if you have already uninstalled the app.

First: cancel your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store; the store-side subscription continues even after your account is deleted, and you may keep being charged.

Cancel your subscription before requesting deletion:

  • Android: Google Play app → profile icon → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → maShop → Cancel subscription
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → maShop → Cancel Subscription

If you start deletion in the app while a subscription is active, maShop warns you about this separately.

Option 1 — From inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open maShop and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the danger zone at the bottom of the page
  4. Tap Delete My Account
  5. Type SİL in the confirmation box and confirm

You will see a confirmation message once your request is received.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app (you changed your phone, uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in), you can send your request by email:

fatihaci79@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request — maShop
  • The email address registered to your account
  • One sentence stating that you are requesting account deletion

Please send the request from the email address registered to the account so we can verify your identity. Requests are completed within 30 days at the latest, in line with Article 13 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698).

What happens after a deletion request?

Stage Duration What happens
Recovery window 30 days Your account is closed but your data is retained. Signing in again during this window restores your account.
Permanent deletion After 30 days All data belonging to your account is permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

Permanently deleted: your name, email address, account identifier, all business records (sales, purchases, expenses, customers, products, staff, commissions), your subscription status record, and the device identifier used to deliver notifications.

The only record kept: an audit entry showing that a deletion took place (deletion date and number of records removed). It is retained under KVKK Articles 7 and 12 and contains no name, email address, or content data.

Separately, records you delete individually inside the app are kept on the server with a "deleted" marker for 30 days so the deletion can reach your other devices, then permanently removed. During that period the record is not displayed anywhere and is excluded from reports.

If you are an invited user (partner / employee)

If you joined someone else's shop account by invitation, Delete My Account removes only your sign-in and email address. The sales records you created remain in the shop, because they are the shop owner's business data. To have those records deleted, contact the shop owner.

If you never sign in

If no one signs in to your account for one continuous year, your account and all its data are permanently deleted without waiting for a request. You are notified in the app before deletion. Signing in resets this period.
